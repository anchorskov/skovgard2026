## Privacy Policy – Skovgard for Senate

**Effective Date:** September 3, 2025

Skovgard for Senate respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.  

### Information We Collect
- Contact information you provide directly (name, email, phone, address).  
- Voter registration data provided by the State of Wyoming.  
- Communications you have with our campaign, including SMS/text messages.  

### How We Use Your Information
- To send you campaign updates, election information, and volunteer opportunities.  
- To respond to your questions or requests.  
- To comply with election law and reporting requirements.  

### Sharing Your Information
We **do not sell or share** your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers who support our campaign operations (e.g., texting platform, payment processor), and only for campaign-related use.  

### Your Choices
- You may opt out of SMS/text messages at any time by replying **STOP**.  
- You may request to update or delete your contact information by emailing us at **anchorskov@gmail.com**.  

### Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.  

### Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:  
**Skovgard for Senate**  
5685 Hanly Mills Wy, Casper, WY 82604  
Email: anchorskov@gmail.com
