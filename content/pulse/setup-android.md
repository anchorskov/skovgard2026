# Pulse Setup – Android

Follow these steps to get started with **Pulse** on your Android device:

---

## Step 1 — Sign Up
Make sure you’ve completed the [Pulse Sign-Up Form](/pulse/signup/).

---

## Step 2 — Save the Campaign Number
When you receive your first campaign text, save the number in your contacts as **Pulse – Skovgard for Senate**.

---

## Step 3 — Forward Campaign Updates
When a Pulse update arrives:
1. Tap and hold the message.  
2. Select **Forward**.  
3. Choose contacts you personally know and trust.  

Always include the opt-out line:  
*Reply STOP to opt out.*

---

## Step 4 — Keep It Personal
Use your own words when you forward. Share why the message matters to you. That personal touch makes all the difference.
