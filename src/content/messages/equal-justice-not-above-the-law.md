## Equal justice, not above the law

My campaign for the United States Senate began in Casper with a commitment to help restore integrity, character, and equal justice to our government. In this message I draw on a stark moment from an early meeting with Dr. Joe McGinley to highlight how powerful institutions continue to delay full transparency for the victims of Jeffrey Epstein's sex-trafficking operation. Evidence and due process must always govern, but no amount of wealth, position, or political influence should ever place anyone above the law or shield them from accountability.

## The politics of powerlessness

Many people tell me they feel powerless. That feeling of insignificance isn't an accident. It's the intended outcome of a political system driven by manufactured outrage and constant division. Our government receives its authority from the people, not the other way around, and silence only serves concentrated power. By combining technology to listen across Wyoming with simple, honest conversations between neighbors, we can overcome that division and reclaim our collective voice.

## Take ownership of our democracy

Every citizen has a role to play: register to vote, and bring a neighbor along. Whether you register at the county clerk's office, by mail, or at the polling place on Election Day, real change doesn't come from high-priced ad buys. It comes from one person standing up alongside another. By demanding equal justice together, we can make sure our votes and our voices become impossible to ignore.
