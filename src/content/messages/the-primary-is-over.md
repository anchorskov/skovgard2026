A campaign can end on a Tuesday in August. Accountability does not. Anyone seeking to hold one of Wyoming's federal offices must answer to Wyoming. That principle becomes real only when the rest of us pay attention.

I ran because I believe Wyoming deserves representation that shows up, answers plainly, and can explain its own votes without hiding behind a press release. I still believe that. Losing a primary does not change what the job requires. It does not change who answers for how the job gets done.

So this is what comes next.

Not a grievance list. A record.

Harriet Hageman and Chuck Gray are asking Wyoming for public trust. The votes each casts, the questions each declines to answer, the promises each makes, and the actions each takes belong in the public record. That record belongs to us, you and me.

My commitment is simple: gather the record, verify it, link to the original sources, and put it before you plainly so you can judge for yourself.

## What we will send you

The record, with links to original sources. Plain-language summaries of decisions that reach your family, your land, your water, and your freedom. Surveys that ask what you actually think, followed by results reported honestly.

## What we will not do

We will not dress opinion up as fact. When a statement reflects my judgment rather than the documented record, it will say so. When new evidence changes the record, we will update it. When we get something wrong, we will correct it.

Wyoming does not need anyone telling us what to think. We deserve the truth and the freedom to decide.
