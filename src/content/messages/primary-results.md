**The sample-ballot idea has grown.**

Before the primary, [candidates.skovgard2026.org](https://candidates.skovgard2026.org)
helped Wyoming voters enter an address, see the races on their ballot, and
learn who was running.

That same resource now brings together primary election results from
22 of Wyoming's 23 counties, with more being added as counties finish
certifying.

Browse federal, statewide, legislative, county, municipal, and precinct
committee races in one place. Select a race to see the candidates, vote
percentages, and current leaders.

The work is not finished. Several counties have not yet published final
certified results, so the site will continue to be updated as final county
data becomes available.
