The economy may look strong on paper, but printed money and runaway spending show up at the grocery store, the gas pump, the utility bill, and the cost of a home or vehicle.

America keeps entering conflicts without a clear path out — open-ended wars that cost lives, treasure, and the trust that holds a nation together.

And the debt keeps climbing. We went from $36 trillion to nearly $40 trillion in less than two years. Elected officials who abdicate their responsibility hand the bill to our grandchildren.

The answer starts with accountability: representatives from the people, not tied to big money, and willing to speak honestly about what is happening.
