Four Pillars

Stronger Together

Cooperation is not weakness — it is how free people solve hard problems. The founding generation, the Union, the Greatest Generation: when we stand together, we succeed.

Modernizing Oversight

Plain English bill summaries. Transparency for digital government systems. Town hall tools to gather and verify Wyoming’s voice where it counts.

Accountability & Term Limits

A term limits amendment. Transparency for money in politics. Oversight and ethics enforcement that has real teeth — not just press releases.

The Constitutional Guardrail

Oath over office. Restore Article I. The Tenth Amendment: powers not delegated remain with the states and the people — including Wyoming.

Born and Raised in Wyoming

Great-grandfather Simon homesteaded near Basin in 1909. Grew up in Greybull and Basin. Educated in Wyoming, from grade school to the University of Wyoming.

Served in the Wyoming Army National Guard, where duty, discipline, and service became part of who I am. My oath to support and defend still stands.

Zero political connections. Zero outside agendas. Just a clean slate and one goal: your voice in the United States Senate.
