# Photography — Skovgard 2026

Last updated: 2026-04-12

---

## Guiding principle

Photography should look Wyoming-real and frontier-grounded, not studio-slick.
Authentic over polished. Durable over delicate. Real places over generic
campaign backdrops.

---

## Settings that work

- Fence lines, ranch gates, gravel roads, stock trailers
- Main street storefronts, diners, coffee shops, machine shops
- Community halls, VFW spaces, fairgrounds, town halls
- Workshops, garages, small business interiors
- Open range, mountains, rivers, irrigation ditches, windbreaks
- Two-lane highways, county roads, practical outdoor settings

## Settings to avoid

- Studio portraits with dramatic theatrical lighting
- DC-looking interiors, marble halls, or generic conference rooms
- Overstaged Western props or costume styling
- Anything that looks like stock photography
- Flag-draped merch-table aesthetics

---

## People in photos

- Jimmy with real people in real places
- Eye contact with the person he is speaking to, not mugging for camera
- Mixed ages and real Wyoming context: families, workers, veterans, ranchers,
  teachers, small business owners
- Handshakes, conversation, walking, listening, explaining
- Avoid stage-above-crowd hero shots unless the event format truly requires it

---

## Landscape / Wyoming shots

- Big sky matters. Distance and horizon matter.
- Use Wyoming landscapes as hero backgrounds, dividers, or social backplates.
- Color treatment can warm slightly toward bone, ember, and sandstone, but do
  not over-filter or make landscapes look sepia.
- Avoid generic mountain stock that could be Colorado, Montana, or Utah.

---

## Technical guidance

- Minimum resolution: 1600px wide for web heroes, 1200x630 for OG images
- Formats: WebP preferred for web, JPEG fallback, PNG only for logos/graphics
- Alt text: descriptive, specific, location-aware when known
- File naming: lowercase, hyphens, descriptive
- Crop for strength: horizon, hands, tools, posture, and real context matter

Good alt text:
"Jimmy Skovgard talking with a rancher beside a stock trailer in Sheridan County"

Bad alt text:
"Campaign photo"

---

## The Bucking Horse mark

Do NOT use the Wyoming Bucking Horse and Rider trademark in campaign materials.
It is a registered state mark with restricted usage and implies state
endorsement. Use Wyoming landscapes and campaign-specific graphics instead.
