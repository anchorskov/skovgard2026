# Voice — Skovgard 2026

Last updated: 2026-04-12

---

## How Jimmy sounds

Jimmy sounds like a Wyoming neighbor who has done hard things and does not need
to posture about it. Direct. Plain-spoken. Grounded. Comfortable with strong
language when the point requires it, but never theatrical.

This frontier direction is firmer than the abandoned civic concept, but it is
still human. The voice is not online-angry, not consultant-polished, and not
cowboy cosplay. It sounds like someone you would trust at the fence line, in a
shop bay, or over coffee after a town meeting.

---

## Word choices

### USE

- Wyoming, neighbors, community, straight talk
- show up, work, earn, serve, duty, responsibility
- home, roots, land, water, jobs, family
- common sense, accountability, real answers
- grit, backbone, steady hand, local control

### AVOID

- slam, crush, destroy, blast, humiliate, wipe out
- consultant language: leverage, stakeholder, ecosystem, lane, narrative
- performative culture-war phrasing and internet grievance slang
- anything that makes Jimmy sound like a pundit instead of a candidate
- "cowboy" as self-branding language unless it is literally relevant to the story

### HANDLE WITH CARE

- "conservative" — use when tied to a concrete value or policy, not as filler
- "fighter" — only when grounded in action or persistence, not chest-thumping
- "outsider" — better to show this through biography than repeat the label
- "patriot" — let service, setting, and conduct carry it unless context requires it

---

## Sentence style

- Short. Active voice. Subject-verb-object.
- One idea per sentence when possible.
- Lead with the point. Context follows.
- If a sentence needs a semicolon, it is two sentences.
- Paragraphs should usually be 2-4 sentences.

---

## Headline style

- Bitter, bold, readable from a distance.
- Declarative, not cute.
- Stronger than the civic version, but still controlled.
- No exclamation marks unless quoting someone.
- Good direction: "Who Actually Shows Up." "In Service to Wyoming." "Straight Talk for Wyoming."

---

## Attribution and quotes

- Jimmy quotes should sound spoken, not speechwritten.
- Endorsement quotes should sound like the endorser, not like campaign copy.
- Photo captions are short, specific, and place-based when possible.
- Avoid invented folklore or mythology language. Keep it real.

## Tone by context

| Context                | Tone |
|------------------------|------|
| Homepage / About       | Warm, sturdy, inviting. Big-sky confidence. |
| Issue pages            | Direct, factual, plain. Respect the reader. |
| Donate / volunteer     | Clear, grateful, low-drama. |
| Rapid response         | Firm, controlled, factual. No heat for heat's sake. |
| Social media           | Brief, human, a little sharper than web copy. |
| Event materials        | Bigger and bolder, but still grounded. |
